INTELLIGENT AIR CARE + PLANT CARE

Cleaner air. Balanced humidity. Automatic care for natural plants.

Viosimos is a concept for a 2-in-1 HEPA air purifier and evaporative humidifier with an automated planter. It is designed for health-conscious homes, allergy-aware adults and first-time plant parents who want better indoor comfort without adding more disconnected appliances.

THE EVERYDAY REALITY

Separate appliances can work well and still make a room feel disconnected

BEFORE — three separate objects

Most people arrange a humidifier, air purifier and plant pot as neatly as they can. Different shapes, finishes and cords can still create visual clutter or make the space feel less intentional.

AFTER — one Viosimos

Viosimos brings the air purifier, humidifier and planter into one coordinated object designed to feel intentional in the room.

THE VIOSIMOS SOLUTION

Viosimos is being developed as one intelligent system for three everyday needs: filtering airborne particles, maintaining comfortable humidity, and supporting consistent plant care. A natural plant benefits from automated watering based on a user-defined schedule and year-round grow lighting, while an artificial arrangement can be used instead for a bolder, lower-maintenance aesthetic. The goal is to deliver cleaner indoor air, balanced humidity, and healthier plants by helping users maintain a consistent watering routine without the need to purchase, arrange, and manage three separate products.

HOW IT WORKS

Three functions, coordinated by one system

01PURIFY

Reduce the particles circulating through your room

A washable pre-filter is planned to capture larger debris before air passes through a HEPA filter designed to reduce pollen, household dust, pet dander and other airborne particles. Final room coverage and performance will be confirmed through prototype testing.

02HUMIDIFY

Keep humidity comfortable—not desert dry or greenhouse damp

The concept uses evaporative humidification with automatic humidity sensing. It is designed to add invisible water vapour before the room becomes uncomfortably dry, slow or pause near the selected range, alert the user when humidity is unusually high and provide a low-water warning.

03NURTURE

Make plant ownership easier from the first day

The removable planter is intended to support selected natural plants through automated watering and plant-care profiles. A grow-light setting would support plant growth through darker seasons. For users who prefer a stronger decorative statement or less maintenance, the planter can be replaced with an artificial arrangement and the light becomes adjustable ambience.

DEVELOPMENT MILESTONES

See where Viosimos is today—and what comes next

Viosimos is being developed through a structured product journey. The current stage is open so early supporters can understand the decisions being tested before industrial design begins.

Current stage · 01

Concept Validation

We are confirming that Viosimos solves a meaningful problem before committing to industrial design and engineering.

What problem are we validating?

Many households use a separate purifier, humidifier and planter. Viosimos tests whether combining those functions into one coordinated product creates enough value in health, comfort, plant care and interior design.

Why HEPA filtration?

The current concept uses HEPA filtration because it is designed to reduce airborne particles such as pollen, household dust and pet dander. Final performance will be established during prototype testing.

Why evaporative humidification?

Evaporative humidification adds invisible water vapour while helping the system avoid the visible mist and mineral dust associated with some ultrasonic designs. Humidity sensing would help prevent the room from becoming too dry or too damp.

How will natural-plant automation work?

The planter is part of the same Viosimos product. The concept explores moisture monitoring, controlled watering, plant-care profiles and scheduled grow lighting, with internal water management kept separate from the humidifier for hygiene and plant health.

What are we asking early supporters?

We are learning which benefit matters most, whether natural or artificial plants are preferred, which smart features people value, where the product would be placed and what price range feels realistic.

Your answers help determine the product size, plant capacity, controls, appearance, maintenance experience and prototype priorities.
