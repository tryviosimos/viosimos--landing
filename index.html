Designed to belong

One object. Four spaces.

Designed for the places where people live, rest and work.

The problem

Indoor comfort has become unnecessarily complicated.

People often need several disconnected products to improve air quality, humidity and plant care — each taking up space and demanding attention.

Too many separate appliances

Purifiers, humidifiers and plant-care accessories compete for outlets, floor space and maintenance time.

Functional, but visually disconnected

Many indoor-care products look like appliances first and interior objects second.

Natural plants require consistency

Lighting and watering routines can be difficult for new or busy plant owners.

The solution

Three functions. One considered object.

Viosimos brings air care and plant care together in a single interior-led concept.

01
Cleaner air

HEPA filtration is intended to help capture airborne particles such as dust, pollen and pet dander.

02
Balanced humidity

Evaporative humidification is designed to support comfortable indoor humidity without visible mist.

03
Automatic plant care

Integrated watering and plant-support lighting are intended to make natural plant ownership easier.

Everyday value

Built around how people actually live.

Less visual clutter

One coordinated footprint replaces several disconnected objects.

More comfortable indoor air

Air filtration and humidity support are considered together rather than separately.

Simpler plant care

Integrated support helps reduce the routine burden of watering and lighting.

Designed for modern interiors

The concept is intended to feel like functional décor, not another appliance.

How it works

Four systems working as one.

The final architecture will be refined through engineering, prototyping, testing and certification.

01

Air intake + HEPA

Air is drawn through a replaceable filtration system designed to capture airborne particles.

02

Evaporative humidity

A water-fed evaporative element supports humidity without producing a visible plume.

03

Watering system

A separated reservoir and controlled delivery system are intended to support plant watering.

04

Plant-support light

Integrated upper lighting supports plant growth while also providing ambient illumination.
