THE IDEA

Viosimos is built on one belief: air care shouldn't look like hardware. It merges humidification and purification with a living plant element — artificial or water-rooted natural — so a room gets cleaner, more comfortable air and a companion that feels alive, not another appliance to hide.

HOW IT WORKS

What it actually does

LIGHT LAYER

Light that breathes with the room

A ring of ambient LEDs sits above the plant, softly brightening and dimming to show humidity level and system status at a glance, while a low-intensity grow spectrum supports whatever is planted at the centre.

PLANT LAYER

A display cradle, not an afterthought

The centre cradle holds either an artificial arrangement or a water-rooted natural plant. Mist rises directly through the foliage before dispersing into the room, so the plant is part of the humidification path, not just decoration beside it.

Artificial arrangement Water-rooted natural plant
WATER LAYER

Fill from the top, forget the mess

The reservoir fills from a wide top opening with a shaped lip that resists splashing. Below it, a washable purification layer and ultrasonic diffuser turn tap water into fine, clean mist without boiling or fogging the room.
