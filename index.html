What it actually does

Three things happen quietly in the background so your room stays comfortable — no dials, no maintenance schedule to remember.

Reads the room
Intelligent environmental sensors track humidity and air quality in real time, so Viosimo knows before you do that the air's gotten dry.
Adjusts itself
Automatic humidity control and air purification respond to what the sensors find — set it once, and it keeps the room in range on its own.
Stays out of the way
Quiet, energy-efficient operation and an easy, low-maintenance refill mean it asks very little of you between fills.
Checks in, if you want it to
Smart app connectivity gives you the readouts and controls on your phone, but Viosimo runs perfectly well if you never open the app.
Built to last, not to look built
Sustainable premium materials and a plant-inspired form mean it reads as a piece of furniture first, a device second.
